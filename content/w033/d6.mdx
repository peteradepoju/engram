<Step n={3} title="Derivation set" minutes={10} />

<Prove title="1. The peak of a difference of exponentials" task="Show that $e^{-t/\tau_d} - e^{-t/\tau_r}$, with $\tau_d \gt \tau_r$, peaks at $t^* = \frac{\tau_r\tau_d}{\tau_d - \tau_r}\ln\frac{\tau_d}{\tau_r}$.">

The derivative is $-\frac{1}{\tau_d}e^{-t/\tau_d} + \frac{1}{\tau_r}e^{-t/\tau_r}$. Setting it to zero gives $\frac{1}{\tau_r}e^{-t/\tau_r} = \frac{1}{\tau_d}e^{-t/\tau_d}$.

Taking logarithms, $-\ln\tau_r - t/\tau_r = -\ln\tau_d - t/\tau_d$, so $t\left(\frac{1}{\tau_r} - \frac{1}{\tau_d}\right) = \ln\frac{\tau_d}{\tau_r}$.

Since $\frac{1}{\tau_r} - \frac{1}{\tau_d} = \frac{\tau_d - \tau_r}{\tau_r\tau_d}$, the peak is at $t^* = \frac{\tau_r\tau_d}{\tau_d - \tau_r}\ln\frac{\tau_d}{\tau_r}$.

</Prove>

<Prove title="2. The limiting frequency" task="For a depressing synapse driven at rate $f$, show that at high rates the steady response per spike is about $A_{max}/(f\tau_{rec})$, so the transmitted amount per second tends to $A_{max}/\tau_{rec}$, independent of both $f$ and $U$.">

With $E = e^{-\Delta/\tau}$ and $\Delta = 1/f$ small, $E \approx 1 - \Delta/\tau$, so $1 - E \approx \Delta/\tau$ and $1 - (1 - U)E \approx U + (1 - U)\Delta/\tau \approx U$ when $\Delta/\tau \ll U$.

Then $x_{ss} = \frac{1 - E}{1 - (1 - U)E} \approx \frac{\Delta}{U\tau}$, and the response per spike is $A_{max}Ux_{ss} \approx A_{max}\Delta/\tau = A_{max}/(f\tau)$.

Multiplying by $f$ spikes per second gives $A_{max}/\tau_{rec}$. The approximation needs $\Delta/\tau \ll U$, that is $f \gg 1/(U\tau_{rec})$: the limiting frequency.

</Prove>
